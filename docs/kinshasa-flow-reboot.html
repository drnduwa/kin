Marketing meeting · proposal · Nov 2026

Kinshasa Flow, rebuilt as a game Kinois play to beat the traffic

Today the app has 411 users in a city of 27 million. People don't trust its numbers, it rarely explains why a road is blocked, and it is buried under 15 unrelated modules. This proposal keeps one promise, "get me out of traffic now", makes every number honest and live, and turns drivers into players whose reports keep the map fresh. What we found in the code

Why the current app doesn't feel accurate

The last release added a "command center", but most of what it calls live is an estimate. Users notice when the app says 25 minutes and the trip takes an hour.

Biggest trust issue

Estimates are shown as live

When Google Routes doesn't answer, travel time is the empty-road time multiplied by a fixed rush-hour factor (2.1 to 3.1). That is a guess, but the screen still labels it as live telemetry.

src/app/actions.ts:1285–1296
"Why" is missing

Causes are fixed sentences

Each bottleneck has one hard-coded cause ("minibus en double file"…) that is shown every time unless a citizen report happens to be nearby. Rain, events, power cuts and accidents never appear.

src/app/actions.ts:1333 onward (defaultCause)
Forecast bug

History is saved at the wrong hour

The traffic logger stores getHours() from the server clock. Firebase servers run on UTC, one hour behind Kinshasa, so the forecast learns that 17:00 traffic happens at 16:00.

src/app/actions.ts:274, 850 · api/cron/traffic-logger/route.ts
Made-up alternatives

Detours aren't measured

The delay on an alternative road is set to 60% of the main road's delay. Nobody checks whether the detour is actually clear.

src/app/actions.ts:972
Focus

Nine traffic screens, fifteen side modules

Traffic is split across verifier-trafic, live-traffic, local-traffic, routes, map, hazard-map, k-flow-nav, insights and flux-infrastructure, next to tourism, housing, car rental and restaurants. New users can't find the one thing they came for.

src/components/app-shell.tsx menu
Cold start

411 users can't cover 27 million people

A Waze-style crowd map needs density. Without a plan to seed data (fleets, paid sentinels, GPS from every app session), most streets show nothing and people uninstall.

The game

One loop: playing makes the map better

DriveOpen the app for your commute. It tells you when to leave and which way.
Report or confirmOne tap: "still blocked" or "it's clear". Three seconds, usable by a passenger.
Earn Stars + XPPaid only after two other people confirm. Streaks and commune battles add pride.
Get rewardsData bundles and airtime through the existing e-wallet, funded by sponsors.
Better mapMore fresh reports → better routes → more people open the app.

Ranks use Kinshasa words people already joke with: Piéton → Wewa → Taximan → Chauffeur Esprit de Mort → Pilote de Kin → Mokonzi ya Nzela. The headline number on every profile is time saved this month, because that is what people will share on WhatsApp.

Businesses and growth

How it pays, and how it reaches people beyond 411

Kin Flow Pro for fleets

A live dashboard for delivery companies, banks, NGOs, logistics and ride-hailing: where each vehicle is, realistic ETAs, and alerts before drivers enter a jam. Monthly fee per vehicle. Their GPS also feeds the map.

Sponsored rewards

Brands (telecoms, fuel stations, banks, beverages) fund the airtime and data prizes and the weekly commune battle. Ads appear only when the car is stopped, never while moving.

Traffic reports

Anonymous monthly reports per corridor for the city, insurers, real-estate developers and investors deciding where to open a shop.

Corridor-first launch

Start with four commutes (UPN, Masina, Lemba, Kintambo → Gombe). Recruit 100 paid "Sentinelles", moto-taxi drivers paid in airtime, so every corridor has fresh data from day one.

Reach people without the app

A WhatsApp bot ("écris LIMETE") and a short daily voice flash in Lingala that radios can play. Both end with "download Kin Flow for your own route".

Shareable wins

After each smart trip, a share card: "J'ai évité 34 min de bouchon à Victoire". Every share is free advertising in the right neighbourhood.

Plan

90 days in three phases

Weeks 1–3 · Make it true

Trust first

  • Fix the UTC hour bug and re-run history
  • Stop showing multiplied estimates as live; show source and confidence
  • Merge the nine traffic screens into the five tabs shown here
  • Hide tourism, housing, rentals, restaurants from the main menu
  • One-tap reporting with confirm / deny
Weeks 4–8 · Make it fun

The game layer

  • XP, levels, ranks, daily missions, streaks
  • Commune battle and driver leaderboards
  • Rewards shop on top of the existing e-wallet
  • Leave-time coach and the "Why" panel (rain, reports, events)
  • Anti-cheat: rewards only after independent confirmations
Weeks 9–13 · Make it scale

Data and reach

  • Opt-in GPS speed probes from every trip
  • First fleet partners and Kin Flow Pro dashboard
  • Sentinelle program on the four corridors
  • WhatsApp bot and Lingala radio flash
  • First sponsor for the reward pool
